    The 7 MSNR Structural Level Types

    1. Resistance (Sell POI)

    Previous swing High where selling pressure overwhelmed buyers.

    2. Support (Buy POI)

    Previous swing Low where buying pressure defended price.

    3. RBS (Resistance Becomes Support)

    Price breaks above resistance, then pulls back to test the old ceiling as a new floor (BUY).

    4. SBR (Support Becomes Resistance)

    Price breaks below support, then pulls back to test the old floor as a new ceiling (SELL).

    5. OCL (Open-Close Level)

    Major candle open/close horizontal alignment representing institutional equilibrium.

    6. QM (Quasimodo Pattern)

    Over-and-Under structure breaking structural swing; high-probability retest of the left shoulder.

    7. Engulfing x Strong Order Block

    An institutional order block that executed a prior liquidity sweep before printing an aggressive engulfing candle.

    Golden Strategy Mandate: Clearing Inducements

    Inducements are bait created by Market Makers to trap early retail traders. Entering before Inducement is cleared has a severe failure rate. Always wait for the TS (Target Sweep) wick to grab retail stops before executing!
